    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

    To convert Celsius to Fahrenheit, multiply the temperature in Celsius by 9/5, then add 32. For example: 0°C = 32°F (freezing point of water), 100°C = 212°F (boiling point). Use our temperature converter for instant conversions.

    How many kilograms are in a pound?

    1 pound (lb) equals approximately 0.4536 kilograms (kg). So 1 kg ≈ 2.2046 pounds. Our weight converter provides instant kg to lbs conversions with precision.

    How many centimeters are in an inch?

    1 inch (in) equals exactly 2.54 centimeters (cm). So 1 cm = 0.3937 inches. Our length converter handles all metric and imperial length conversions instantly.

    How many liters are in a gallon?

    1 US gallon equals 3.7854 liters. So 1 liter ≈ 0.2642 gallons. Use our volume converter for quick conversions between liters, gallons, milliliters, and more.
