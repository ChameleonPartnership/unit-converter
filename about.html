About Unit Converter

Unit Converter is a free, lightweight web calculator designed for everyday metric and imperial conversions. It supports temperature, length, weight, volume, pressure, speed, and area conversions without requiring a page reload or account signup.

Our Mission

We believe unit conversion should be fast, simple, and private. That's why we built a converter that runs entirely in your browser with zero tracking, no advertisements cluttering the interface, and no data collection.

How It Works

All calculations run locally in your browser using standard international conversion factors (SI units). Your recent conversion history and dark mode preferences are stored only in your browser's localStorage—we never send data to our servers or third parties.

Key Features

  • Dark Mode: Easy on the eyes during nighttime use
  • Copy & Share: One-click copying and native sharing
  • Conversion History: Access your last 5 conversions
  • Accessibility First: WCAG 2.1 compliant with full keyboard navigation
  • Offline Ready: Works without internet after initial load
  • Privacy Focused: No tracking, no analytics, no cookies

Supported Conversions

Temperature (Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin) • Length (mm, cm, m, km, inches, feet, yards, miles) • Weight (mg, g, kg, oz, lb, ton) • Volume (ml, l, fl oz, cup, pint, gallon) • Pressure (Pa, kPa, bar, psi, atm) • Speed (m/s, km/h, mph, knots) • Area (mm², cm², m², km², in², ft², acres)

Accuracy

Our converter uses precise international unit definitions and mathematical formulas for accurate results. All conversions are calculated with up to 12 significant figures of precision.
